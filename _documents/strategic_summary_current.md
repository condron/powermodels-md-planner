# PowerModels — Strategic Summary (Current)
**As of:** 2026-02-14  
**Purpose:** Reconcile planning overview and business plan. Define current strategy, dropped ideas, and phased roadmap.

---

## Executive Summary

PowerModels is a **financial modeling platform** with tax preparation as the **leading wedge to revenue**. The strategic play is a platform that grows in the market, adding features and revenue streams over time. The company is executing a **disciplined, phased strategy**:

- **Phase 1 (Tax Prep Wedge - April 2026)**: Cloud-based tax preparation, association-driven distribution, rapid onboarding via QuickBooks/Xero
- **Phase 2+ (Platform Expansion)**: Expand modeling capabilities, additional revenue streams, broader feature set

**Key Insight**: Tax preparation is the entry point, not the end goal. The business plan represents the wedge strategy; the planning overview represents the platform vision. Both are valid and sequenced.

**April 2026 Launch**: Cloud-based go-to-market (pivot from local-first architecture).

## Current Strategy (Phase 1)

### Product Focus (Strategic Vision)
- **Financial modeling platform** (long-term strategic play)
- **Tax preparation as leading wedge** (entry to revenue, not end goal)
- Platform grows by adding features and revenue streams over time
- Event-sourced architecture: rewind/refresh/replay without rebuilds
- Eliminate reconciliation structurally

### Phase 1 Product (Tax Prep Wedge)
- Prepare books to **85% completion** (professional judgment remains at 15%)
- Entry via **tax season forcing function** (can't be deferred)
- Build trust and adoption through successful tax season
- Foundation for expanding to broader modeling capabilities

### Go-To-Market
- **Firm-first ONLY** (no direct SMB channel in Phase 1)
- Sell to **accounting firms**, expand across their client portfolios
- Leveraged unit economics: CAC per firm, revenue per client
- Pricing: $50-150/entity/month (firm-licensed, per-client subscription)

### Distribution Channels (Phase 1)
- **Accounting associations** (B2B2B channel - association → firms → clients)
- Association recommended product status
- CPE presentations to reach small accounting firms
- **Associations don't use the product** - they provide distribution access to member firms
- Peer network effects and top-of-funnel trust

### Market Positioning
- **Event-sourced architecture** + **AI empowerment** as dual differentiators
- "AI to empower accountants, not replace them"
- Trust through auditability, lineage, reproducibility
- Professional preparation (not automation replacement)
- Single financial record eliminates drift

### Success Criteria (Phase 1)
- Prove tax preparation to 85% works (wedge validated)
- Validate firm adoption and expansion mechanics
- Demonstrate unit economics (CAC payback, expansion revenue)
- Rapid onboarding via QuickBooks/Xero integration
- Association-driven distribution proven
- Revenue flowing from tax prep wedge
- Foundation established for platform feature expansion
