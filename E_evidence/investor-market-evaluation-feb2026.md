## Market Environment (Feb 2026)

### What Investors Reward
1. **Workflow-embedded B2B** with crisp wedge and credible distribution
2. **Trust + auditability** in AI-adjacent finance products (buyers want guardrails)
3. **Capital efficiency signals** (short CAC payback, onboarding scalability, burn discipline)

### What Investors Punish
1. **"AI will do accounting" positioning** (credibility gap + professional backlash)
2. **Services creep** without software margins
3. **Heroic onboarding** that won't scale

### PowerModels Alignment
**Strong alignment with rewarded signals**:
- "Accountant-hosted" not "replace accountant"
- "Explicit uncertainty" (85/15 boundary)
- "Proven completeness" with professional guardrails
- Matches broader industry direction per Accounting Today

## Investor Concerns (Must Address)

### 1. "Proven Completeness" Needs Crisp Definition
**Current state**: Compelling but abstract
**Investor questions**:
- Completeness against what? (Bank/CC only? Cash-basis? Accrual?)
- What's the acceptance test? (Measurable criteria?)
- What's the failure mode? (Missing statements, duplicates, ambiguous merchants?)

**Required**: One-page "Completeness Contract" with pass/fail criteria

**Updated answer (based on strategy)**:
- **Input**: QB/Xero transaction history
- **Process**: Event-sourced import + AI categorization
- **Output**: 85% prepared books, 15% flagged for review
- **Validation**: Reconciliation to source platform + CPA sign-off
- **Failure modes**: Missing bank feeds, duplicate imports, ambiguous categories

### 2. Roadmap Gates Are Stale
**Problem**: Gate 1 listed as Jan 15, 2026 (already past)
**Investor perception**: Immediate doubt about execution

**Fix required**: Present as current status with proof artifacts
- Gate 1: Achieved / In progress / Slipped (with demo, runbook, test data)
- Gate 2: What's left + evidence you'll show

**Updated gates (aligned to April 2026 launch)**:
- **Gate 1 (Feb 2026)**: Cloud migration + Xero connector completion
- **Gate 2 (March 2026)**: Association partnerships + CPE materials ready
- **Gate 3 (April 2026)**: Cloud launch with 2-3 pilot firms through associations

### 3. Pricing — First Validation (UPDATED Feb 2026)
**Stated pricing**: $50-150/entity/month (business plan)
**First validation**: $20/mo/client confirmed by George Bancroft (beta user)

**Key findings:**
- $20/mo matches QuickBooks cost — a price point clients already accept
- Pass-through model: firm adds to client's monthly bill, same as QB
- Several hundred clients × $20/mo = $4K-6K+ MRR per firm
- This may be the base tier; higher tiers for advanced features

**Remaining need**: Additional pricing data from Stewart and Liberty Tax trials

### 4. Onboarding Capacity is "The Scariest Risk"
**Investor perception**: Correctly identified constraint, no scaling mechanism shown

**Questions investors will ask**:
- What reduces onboarding from 45 → 21 days?
- What % becomes self-serve via templates/runbooks?
- What's target "implementation hours per firm" at scale?

**Problem**: Plan states problem but doesn't show scaling mechanism with numbers

**Answer (based on QB/Xero strategy)**:
- **QuickBooks + Xero integration IS the scaling mechanism**
- Traditional manual onboarding: Days to weeks per client
- QB/Xero import onboarding: Hours to onboard, ready same day
- 10-100x faster client onboarding
- This eliminates the capacity throttle
