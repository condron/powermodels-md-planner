# Design Partner Program and Pilot Validation

## Description

PowerModels uses a structured **design partner program** to validate the product with real accounting firms before scaling. Design partners are ICP-fit firms (50-125 business clients, tax-focused) who pilot the platform through a full tax cycle in exchange for early access, dedicated support, and input into product direction.

## Key Points

- **Target**: 2-4 ICP-fit firms in design partner mode
- **Scope**: 10-30 entities processed end-to-end per firm
- **Timeline**: April-October 2026 (tax season + extensions)
- **Source**: Association membership (PSTAP as first target)
- **Purpose**: Validate preparation workflow, measure time savings, generate proof artifacts

## Design Partner Criteria

### Ideal Firm Profile
- 50-125 business clients (matches PSTAP average)
- 70%+ revenue from tax services
- Currently using QuickBooks Online or Xero
- Willing to share data and provide references
- Partner-level champion committed to pilot

### Pilot Structure
1. **Onboarding** (1-2 days): QB/Xero import, firm setup, training
2. **Tax prep** (April 15 deadline): Process pilot clients through workflow
3. **Extension season** (through October 15): Continue with additional clients
4. **Measurement**: Time savings, accuracy, exception rate, NPS
5. **Case study**: Document results for investor and association materials

## Proof Artifacts (Investor "Proof Room")

- Demo recording (QB/Xero import → 85% prep → CPA review)
- Onboarding runbook (repeatable process documentation)
- Test dataset (anonymized client import for demos)
- Sample working papers package (output quality evidence)
- Measured time-to-first-cycle (onboarding speed metric)
- Firm NPS and testimonials (satisfaction evidence)

## Success Criteria

- Time-to-first-cycle < 1 day (via QB/Xero import)
- Hours saved per client: 40-60% reduction
- NPS > 40 from pilot firm partners
- 2+ firms willing to provide reference or case study
- Pricing validated (LOIs or paid pilot conversion)
