# Customer Interview Table — January 2026

12 real potential customer interviews conducted January 2026. Each row is a short summary from a real firm or individual.

## Key Findings

### Shoebox / Onboarding Pain (strongest signal)
- **Stewart (independent, 3 accountants):** Setting up a new small account can take ~100 hours of bookkeeping setup that cannot be billed back to the client. Must be recouped over time. **Signal: Strong** — explicitly TB- and continuity-driven.
- **Alex (shoebox client):** 4 years of messy data. Wants someone to fix it. Represents the actual end-client pain.
- **Martin (solo):** Paper, receipts, manual intake. Reducing manual effort is the core need.
- **Affirm LLP (23 staff):** Juniors rebuild books annually from statements/CSVs. Data entry, missing feeds, chasing clients.

### Repeat Cleanup / Reconciliation Pain
- **Realm Tax (10-12 staff, 2,000 clients):** "Take what they bring." Commingling, rework, staffing shortages.
- **Ginger (ex-Baker Tilly):** Journal-entry heavy cleanup. Client self-managed QBO, write-offs. Big firms avoid this.
- **Enkel (~100 staff):** Continuous cleanup at scale. Document intake and manual review. Pain is large but buyer is complex.
- **Liberty Tax (Sasha):** Seasonal pressure. Faster reconciliation is the need.

### Completeness / Confidence Pain
- **Accountvisor (2 principals):** Low confidence in completeness. Transfer tracing. Values continuity and confidence. **Signal: Medium-Strong.**
- **Eddie (fractional CFO, 7 staff):** Already solved cleanup with enforced process. Validates the ideal end-state we're building toward.

### Staffing / Scale Pain
- **Innova (Gabrielle, ~250 clients):** Hiring/training bookkeepers, turnover, mistakes, scaling headcount. Standardization, time savings, not hiring more staff. **Signal: Strong** — repeat cleanup + staffing pain + scale mindset + investor interest.
- **Arishma (Liberty Tax Surrey):** QBO-based workflow with heavy manual review. Tax-season compression. **Excellent engine test site** — real data access, shadow-mode pilots feasible.

## Quantified Evidence

- **100 hours** unbillable bookkeeping setup per new small account (Stewart)
- **~250 clients** managed by solo operator with systematized cleanup (Innova/Gabrielle)
- **~2,000 clients** across 10-12 staff with heavy rework (Realm Tax)
- **$2,000-$2,500** bookkeeping package for simple new businesses (Liberty Tax/Arishma)
- **$160-$300** for small business filings like Uber drivers (Liberty Tax/Arishma)
- **~6 companies/year** growth rate at a single Liberty Tax location (Liberty Tax/Arishma)
