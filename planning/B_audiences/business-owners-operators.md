# Business Owners & Operators

## Description

Business owners and operators are the indirect beneficiaries of PowerModels. They don't buy the platform directly (their accounting firm does), but they experience improved financial visibility, faster reporting, and reduced rework when their firm uses standardized preparation.

## Primary Concerns

- **Timely information**: Can I get financial reports faster and more reliably?
- **Trust and accuracy**: Are the numbers right? Will I be surprised later?
- **Cost**: Will this reduce my accounting fees or increase them?
- **Operational impact**: Will this disrupt my business operations or data flows?
- **Usability**: Will reports be clearer and easier to understand?
- **Control**: Do I still have visibility and input into my books?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Business outcomes, not accounting mechanics (cash flow, profitability, decision-ready reports)
- **Proof points**: Testimonials from peer business owners, time-to-close improvements, fewer corrections
- **Format**: Simplified explanations from their accountant, not direct vendor communication
- **Cadence**: Periodic updates through their trusted accounting firm, not direct marketing

## Decision Factors

- **Firm recommendation**: Business owners trust their accountant's tool choice
- **No operational burden**: Cannot require significant time investment or process changes
- **Cost transparency**: Must understand if/how this affects accounting fees
- **Perceived value**: Must see tangible benefit (faster close, better visibility, fewer surprises)
- **Indirect adoption**: Decision is made by their firm, not directly by the business
- **Minimal friction**: Cannot disrupt bank feeds, payroll integrations, or existing workflows
