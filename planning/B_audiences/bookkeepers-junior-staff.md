# Bookkeepers & Junior Staff

## Description

Bookkeepers and junior accounting staff are the **daily users** within firms who perform data entry, transaction categorization, reconciliation, and initial preparation work. They are the hands-on operators who will interact with PowerModels most frequently. Their adoption and comfort level directly determines whether firm-level rollout succeeds.

## Primary Concerns

- **Job security**: Will AI replace my role? Am I being automated out?
- **Workflow disruption**: Will this change how I work day-to-day during busy season?
- **Reliability**: Can I trust the system during tax season when mistakes are costly?
- **Learning curve**: How long before I'm productive? Will it slow me down initially?
- **Clarity**: What did the system do? What do I still need to do? Where are the flagged items?
- **Recognition**: Does the firm value my review work, or just the AI output?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Practical workflow terms (flagged items, review queue, import status, exception list)
- **Proof points**: Hands-on demos, side-by-side workflow comparisons, peer testimonials
- **Format**: Training sessions, quick-reference guides, in-app guidance
- **Cadence**: Pre-season training, in-season support, post-season feedback sessions

## Decision Factors

- **Not a threat**: Must be positioned as tool that makes their job easier, not redundant
- **Clear handoffs**: System must clearly show what it did and what needs human review
- **Workflow fit**: Must integrate into existing daily process without major disruption
- **Training support**: Must include adequate training time before busy season
- **Feedback loop**: Their input on accuracy and usability must be valued and acted on
- **Career growth**: Opportunity to move from data entry to higher-value review work
