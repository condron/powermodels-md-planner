# Angel Investors

## Description

Angel investors provide early-stage capital, often based on founder quality and vision more than traction. They take high risk on unproven ideas, betting on the team's ability to find product-market fit and execute efficiently with limited resources.

## Primary Concerns

- **Founder quality**: Do I trust this team to figure it out and persevere?
- **Problem validity**: Is this a real, painful problem worth solving?
- **Market opportunity**: Is the market big enough to generate venture-scale returns?
- **Scrappiness**: Can this team do more with less? Are they capital efficient?
- **Early validation**: Are there any early signals (customer interest, pilots, LOIs)?
- **Path to next round**: Can they hit milestones to raise a seed/Series A?
- **Personal conviction**: Do I understand and believe in this vision?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Entrepreneurial and problem-focused (customer pain, founder hustle, market opportunity)
- **Proof points**: Founder backgrounds, customer interviews, problem validation, early traction
- **Format**: Pitch decks, informal updates, 1:1 conversations, occasional group updates
- **Cadence**: Monthly or quarterly updates, responsive to founder requests for help

## Decision Factors

- **Founder commitment**: Team working for sweat equity signals conviction
- **Problem depth**: Have they lived this problem? Do they deeply understand it?
- **Capital discipline**: Small checks, long runway, disciplined spending
- **Unique insight**: What do they see that others don't?
- **Network value**: Can I help with intros, advice, or domain expertise?
- **Portfolio fit**: Does this align with my investment thesis and other bets?
