# Board Members

## Description

Board members provide governance, strategic oversight, and accountability. They balance the interests of founders, investors, employees, and other stakeholders while ensuring the company executes on its strategy and manages risk appropriately.

## Primary Concerns

- **Governance and oversight**: Are proper controls and processes in place?
- **Strategic execution**: Is the team hitting milestones? Is the strategy working?
- **Risk management**: What are the top risks and how are they being mitigated?
- **Capital stewardship**: Is capital being deployed efficiently? When is next raise needed?
- **Team performance**: Is leadership executing effectively? Are there gaps?
- **Stakeholder alignment**: Are founder, investor, and employee interests aligned?
- **Exit positioning**: What's the path to liquidity and value creation?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Governance and strategic terminology (fiduciary duty, risk oversight, strategic alternatives)
- **Proof points**: Board materials with KPIs, variance analysis, risk assessment, strategic options
- **Format**: Structured board meetings with pre-reads, executive sessions, committee work
- **Cadence**: Quarterly board meetings, monthly CEO updates, ad-hoc for major decisions

## Decision Factors

- **Informed consent**: Need full transparency on risks, alternatives, and tradeoffs
- **Strategic alignment**: Decisions must align with approved strategy and capital plan
- **Risk vs reward**: Balance growth ambition with prudent risk management
- **Stakeholder fairness**: Ensure decisions don't unfairly favor one stakeholder group
- **Execution capability**: Team must demonstrate they can deliver on commitments
- **Market validation**: External proof points (customer traction, competitive position) matter
