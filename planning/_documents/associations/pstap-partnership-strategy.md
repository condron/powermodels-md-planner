# PSTAP Partnership Strategy

**Association**: Pennsylvania Society of Tax & Accounting Professionals (PSTAP)  
**Status**: Target for first association partnership  
**Timeline**: Engagement Q1 2026, Pilot April-October 2026 (through tax season + extension deadline)

---

## PSTAP Profile

### Membership
- **Members**: ~2,000 practitioners (public practice focus)
- **Member profile**: Small firm niche, tax services focus
- **Client reach**: 200,000-250,000 small businesses
- **Average clients per member**: 100-125 business entities
- **Target clients**: S-Corps, LLCs, small partnerships, individual taxpayers

### Geographic Coverage
- **Region**: 67 Pennsylvania counties
- **Structure**: 9 regional chapters
- **Market type**: "Main Street" businesses (restaurants, retail, professional services, contractors)
- **Employers**: Primarily 1-20 employee businesses

### Strategic Fit
- ✅ **Perfect ICP alignment**: Small firms with 50-200 business entities
- ✅ **Tax-focused**: Matches tax prep wedge strategy
- ✅ **Public practice**: Firm-first GTM alignment
- ✅ **Business entities**: S-Corps, LLCs = PowerModels target market
- ✅ **Regional concentration**: Focused pilot market (Pennsylvania only)

## Partnership Objectives

### Phase 1: Pilot (April-October 2026)

**Primary goals**:
1. Secure **recommended product status** with PSTAP
2. Execute **CPE presentations** in 2-3 regional chapters
3. Onboard **3 pilot firms** from PSTAP membership
4. Process **10-30 entities per pilot firm** through full tax cycle
5. Document **time savings, accuracy improvements, firm satisfaction**

**Success metrics**:
- 3 firms onboarded and active by April 15, 2026
- 30+ client entities processed through tax season
- Measured time-to-first-cycle < 1 day (via QB import)
- NPS > 40 from pilot firm partners
- 2+ firms willing to provide reference/case study

### Phase 2: Expansion (Q4 2026 - Q1 2027)

**Scaling goals**:
1. Expand to **20 PSTAP firms** (1% of membership)
2. Process **2,000+ entities** (10% of firm portfolios)
3. Achieve **$200K MRR** from PSTAP channel
4. Document **repeatable association partnership playbook**
5. Identify **next 2-3 associations** based on PSTAP learnings

## Value Proposition

### For PSTAP (Association)

**Member benefits**:
- Competitive advantage: Faster, more accurate tax preparation
- Practice efficiency: Reduce manual data entry and reconciliation
- Client satisfaction: More time for advisory, less on prep
- Revenue protection: Improve margins on tax prep services

**Association value**:
- Exclusive member benefit (recommended product status)
- CPE content: "AI-Powered Tax Preparation for Small Firms"
- Pennsylvania-focused: Support local small firms
- Non-competitive: PowerModels doesn't serve clients directly

**Positioning**: "Tools that help our members compete and scale"

### For PSTAP Members (Firms)

**Operational benefits**:
- **Rapid client onboarding**: QB/Xero import in hours (vs days of manual entry)
- **AI-powered prep to 85%**: Categorization, reconciliation, flagging
- **Professional control**: CPA reviews and signs off on flagged 15%
- **Event-sourced audit trail**: Complete lineage for every adjustment

**Economic benefits**:
- **Time savings**: 40-60% reduction in tax prep hours per client
- **Capacity expansion**: Handle more clients in same tax season
- **Error reduction**: Automated reconciliation catches issues early
- **Client retention**: Better service, faster turnaround

**Risk mitigation**:
- **Professional judgment preserved**: 85/15 boundary protects CPA role
- **No platform lock-in**: Event-sourced independence after import
- **Trust through auditability**: Full transparency, no black-box AI

### For Firm Clients (End Users)

**Client benefits**:
- Faster tax prep (accountant has more capacity)
- More time for advisory (less spent on data entry)
- Year-round access to prepared records
- Confidence in accuracy (event-sourced audit trail)

**Client experience**: Transparent, accountant-led, no direct PowerModels interaction

## Engagement Strategy

### Step 1: Association Leadership Engagement (Feb 2026)

**Outreach targets**:
- PSTAP Executive Director
- Board members (1-2 key advocates)
- Education Committee (CPE programming)

**Initial pitch**:
- "We're building AI-powered tax preparation for small Pennsylvania firms"
- "Looking for 3 design partners from PSTAP for April 2026 pilot"
- "Offering recommended product status + CPE content in exchange for pilot participation"

**Ask**:
1. Introduction to 5-10 member firms (pilot candidates)
2. CPE presentation slot in 2-3 regional chapters (March-April 2026)
3. Recommended product listing on PSTAP website/materials

**Offer**:
- Free pilot for 3 firms (April-October 2026)
- CPE presentation content (1-2 credit hours)
- Case study and feedback for PSTAP member communications
- Pennsylvania-focused commitment (local small firm support)

### Step 2: CPE Presentation Development (March 2026)

**Title**: "AI-Powered Tax Preparation: The 85/15 Boundary for Small Firm CPAs"

**Content outline**:
1. **Industry reality**: Why AI bookkeeping doesn't work (reference podcast)
2. **The 85/15 approach**: AI to empower, not replace
3. **Live demo**: QB import → 85% prep → CPA review → client-ready
4. **Case study**: PSTAP pilot firm results (if available)
5. **Practice economics**: Time savings, capacity expansion, margin protection

**Delivery**:
- 60-90 minute presentation
- 1-2 CPE credits (varies by chapter)
- Live demo with real (anonymized) data
- Q&A focused on practitioner concerns

**Regional chapters** (target 2-3):
- Philadelphia
- Pittsburgh
- Harrisburg/Central PA

### Step 3: Pilot Firm Selection (March 2026)

**Ideal pilot firm profile**:
- **Size**: 1-3 partners, 50-150 business clients
- **Tax focus**: 70%+ revenue from tax services
- **Technology comfort**: Already using QB Online or Xero
- **Geographic diversity**: Different PSTAP chapters
- **Willingness to share**: Open to case study and reference

**Selection criteria**:
1. Active PSTAP member (2+ years)
2. Strong chapter reputation
3. Tech-forward (not bleeding edge, but willing to try)
4. Diverse client base (S-Corps, LLCs, partnerships)
5. Committed partner-level champion

**Pilot offer**:
- Free through October 2026 (full tax season + extensions)
- Dedicated onboarding support (1:1 training)
- Priority feature requests
- Case study participation ($X discount for 1 year after pilot)

### Step 4: Pilot Execution (April-October 2026)

**Onboarding** (April 1-15):
- Firm setup and training (2-4 hours)
- QB/Xero connection and authorization
- Select 10-30 pilot clients per firm
- Initial import and validation

**Tax season** (April 15 deadline):
- Process clients through tax prep workflow
- Track time savings, accuracy, issues
- Weekly check-ins with pilot firms
- Collect feedback and iterate

**Extension season** (through October 15):
- Continue with extension clients
- Refine workflows based on feedback
- Document case studies and metrics
- Validate expansion mechanics (adding more clients)

**Measurement**:
- Time-to-first-cycle (target: < 1 day via QB import)
- Hours saved per client (target: 40-60%)
- Error/exception rate (track declining trend)
- Firm NPS (target: > 40)
- Client expansion within firm (track growth)

## Risks and Mitigations

### Risk: Association doesn't grant recommended product status
**Mitigation**: Start with CPE access only, build credibility through pilot results

### Risk: Pilot firms don't complete full tax season
**Mitigation**: Select committed firms, provide dedicated support, track engagement weekly

### Risk: April 2026 timeline too aggressive
**Mitigation**: Cloud launch by April 1, onboarding starts April 1-15, deadline is April 15 (tight but achievable)

### Risk: QB/Xero import not working at scale
**Mitigation**: Test with pilot firm data in March, fix issues before tax deadline

### Risk: 85% automation threshold not met
**Mitigation**: Set expectation that pilot is for validation, not perfection; iterate based on feedback

### Risk: Pricing resistance from small firms
**Mitigation**: Emphasize time savings = margin improvement; offer pilot-to-paid discount
