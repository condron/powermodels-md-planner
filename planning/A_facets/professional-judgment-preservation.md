# Professional Judgment Preservation

## Description

PowerModels is explicitly designed to **stop at 85% completion**, preserving the final 15% for professional judgment, review, and sign-off. This boundary is deliberate and structural. The platform automates preparation and eliminates rework, but intentionally does not attempt to replace professional accountability.

## The 85/15 Principle

### Platform Responsibilities (85%)
- **Ingest all transactions** from banks, QuickBooks, other sources
- **Reconcile for completeness** (confirm all activity captured)
- **Categorize confidently classifiable transactions**
- **Maintain persistent schedules** (depreciation, prepaids, deferred revenue)
- **Produce review-ready working papers**
- **Flag uncertain or ambiguous items** explicitly for review

### Professional Responsibilities (15%)
- **Review flagged transactions** and apply judgment
- **Make client-specific adjustments** based on context
- **Interpret tax law and strategy**
- **Sign off on positions and classifications**
- **Communicate with clients** about financial results and tax positions
- **Bear professional liability** for accuracy and compliance

## Why This Boundary Matters

### Preserves Professional Value
- CPAs are paid for judgment, not data entry
- Expertise applied where it matters: interpretation, not categorization
- Professional accountability intact (no services creep)

### Builds Trust with Firms
- Firms won't adopt tools that threaten to replace them
- Preserving the 15% shows respect for professional role
- Platform positioned as leverage, not replacement

### Avoids Services Creep
- PowerModels remains a software tool, not a service provider
- No liability for incorrect tax positions (CPA signs off, not platform)
- Clear separation between automation and professional services

### Enables Adoption
- CPAs can trust the platform without fearing it will make decisions beyond its competence
- Uncertainty surfacing (flagged items) builds confidence—platform knows what it doesn't know

## Explicit Uncertainty Surfacing

When PowerModels encounters:
- **Ambiguous categorizations** → Flag for review
- **Unusual transaction patterns** → Flag for review
- **Missing supporting documentation** → Flag for review
- **Cross-entity or inter-company activity** → Flag for review
- **Items requiring tax interpretation** → Flag for review

This is a **feature, not a bug**. Transparency about uncertainty builds trust.

## Contrast with "Full Automation" Approaches

| Full Automation | PowerModels (85/15) |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Tries to classify everything | Flags ambiguity explicitly |
| Black box decisions | Transparent reasoning |
| Replaces professional | Leverages professional |
| High risk if wrong | Professional reviews flagged items |
| CPAs disengage | CPAs stay engaged on judgment |

## Business Model Alignment

The 85/15 boundary aligns with the **firm-first GTM model**:
- Firms control the final 15% → they remain valuable to clients
- Firms capture efficiency gains from automating the 85% → improved margins
- Clients still rely on firm expertise → retention intact
- Platform doesn't compete with firms → adoption barrier removed

## How This Works in Practice

1. **Platform ingests and categorizes** transactions automatically
2. **Uncertain items flagged** in review queue
3. **CPA reviews flagged items** and applies judgment
4. **CPA makes final adjustments** based on client context
5. **CPA signs off** on completed preparation
6. **Platform refreshes** outputs deterministically based on CPA decisions

The CPA remains in control. The platform eliminates grunt work, not expertise.

## Strategic Importance

This boundary is not just good product design—it's **essential for adoption**. Accounting firms will not adopt tools that:
- Obscure what decisions were made and why
- Attempt to replace professional judgment
- Introduce liability the firm can't control
- Threaten to disintermediate the firm from the client

By stopping at 85%, PowerModels becomes a **force multiplier for professionals**, not a replacement.
