# PowerModels — Strategic Summary (Current)
**As of:** 2026-02-14  
**Purpose:** Reconcile planning overview and business plan. Define current strategy, dropped ideas, and phased roadmap.

---

## Executive Summary

PowerModels is a **financial preparation and reporting platform** that eliminates reconciliation through event-sourced architecture. The company is executing a **disciplined, phased strategy**:

- **Phase 1 (Current)**: Tax preparation wedge, firm-first GTM, accounting preparation to 85%
- **Phase 2+ (Future)**: Expand to forecasting/modeling, add distribution channels, broader market

The business plan (Jan 2026) represents **Phase 1 focus**. The planning overview represents **Phase 2+ vision**. Both are valid, but sequenced.

---

## Current Strategy (Phase 1)

### Product Focus
- **Financial preparation and reporting platform**
- Entry via **tax preparation** (forcing function for correctness)
- Prepare books to **85% completion** (professional judgment remains at 15%)
- Event-sourced architecture: rewind/refresh/replay without rebuilds
- Eliminate reconciliation structurally

### Go-To-Market
- **Firm-first ONLY** (no direct SMB channel in Phase 1)
- Sell to **accounting firms**, expand across their client portfolios
- Leveraged unit economics: CAC per firm, revenue per client
- Pricing: $50-150/entity/month (firm-licensed, per-client subscription)

### Distribution Channels (Phase 1)
- **Accounting firms** (small to mid-size)
- Focus on single channel to prove motion before expansion

### Market Positioning
- **Event-sourced architecture** as primary differentiator
- Trust through auditability, lineage, reproducibility
- Professional preparation (not automation replacement)
- Single financial record eliminates drift

### Success Criteria (Phase 1)
- Prove tax preparation to 85% works
- Validate firm adoption and expansion mechanics
- Demonstrate unit economics (CAC payback, expansion revenue)
- Reach cash-flow breakeven through firm rollout alone

## Phase 1 → Phase 2+ Transition Criteria

### What Must Be True to Expand

**Before adding new channels:**
- 10+ accounting firms actively using platform
- Proven expansion mechanics (firms growing from 5 → 20+ clients)
- CAC payback < 12 months demonstrated
- Retention > 90% at firm level

**Before expanding product scope (forecasting/modeling):**
- Tax preparation to 85% proven reliable
- Event-sourced record trusted by professionals
- Same record successfully reused for reporting (not just tax)
- Customer demand validated for forecasting use cases

**Before launching direct SMB:**
- Firm channel proven and scaling
- Self-service product experience built and tested
- Clear differentiation between firm-served and direct-served customers

## Working Positioning Statement

Built on event-sourced architecture, it enables firms to prepare client books to 85% completion through tax season, then reuse the same trusted record for reporting and forecasting—without rebuilds.

**Future (Phase 2+):**
> PowerModels is a trust-first financial platform that makes accounting firms and finance professionals more scalable. Combining ledger-grade lineage, reproducible models, and partner-delivered solutions, it transforms preparation, reporting, and forecasting into a single, auditable workflow.
