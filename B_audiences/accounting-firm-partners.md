# Accounting Firm Partners

## Description

Firm partners are the economic decision-makers who control technology adoption, service standards, and client relationships. They bear P&L responsibility for the firm and prioritize investments that improve margins, capacity, and quality without increasing risk or overhead.

## Primary Concerns

- **Economic viability**: Will this reduce cost-to-serve and improve margins?
- **Client control**: Does this strengthen or weaken the firm's relationship with clients?
- **Scalability**: Can we expand across our portfolio without linear cost increases?
- **Professional risk**: Does this preserve accountability and defensibility?
- **Competitive positioning**: Does this differentiate us or commoditize our services?
- **Adoption risk**: Will staff resist? Will clients balk at changes?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Business outcomes, not technical features (margin improvement, capacity expansion, client retention)
- **Proof points**: Pilot data from similar firms, time-to-value metrics, client testimonials
- **Format**: Executive summaries with clear economics, risk assessment, and rollout plan
- **Cadence**: Quarterly business reviews, not constant feature updates

## Decision Factors

- **Trust through pilot**: Must prove value with 3-5 high-pain clients before broader rollout
- **Firm control**: Pricing and expansion must be firm-controlled, not per-client negotiation
- **No services creep**: Tool must stop at 85% preparation, preserving professional judgment
- **Integration with existing workflow**: Cannot disrupt tax season or require staff retraining
- **Long-term lock-in concerns**: Must see path to multi-year value, not just seasonal benefit
