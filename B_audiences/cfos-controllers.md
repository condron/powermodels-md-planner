# CFOs & Controllers

## Description

CFOs and Controllers in multi-entity organizations or growing businesses manage financial reporting, consolidation, compliance, and planning. They are sophisticated financial professionals who understand the cost of fragmented systems and value tools that improve accuracy, speed, and auditability.

## Primary Concerns

- **Close efficiency**: Can we reduce days-to-close without sacrificing accuracy?
- **Audit readiness**: Will this improve our audit outcomes and reduce findings?
- **Consolidation**: Can we aggregate across entities without manual Excel gymnastics?
- **Compliance and controls**: Does this strengthen or weaken our internal controls?
- **Forecast accuracy**: Can we produce rolling forecasts without constant rebuilds?
- **Data integrity**: Can we trust the numbers without constant reconciliation?
- **Integration**: Does this work with our existing tech stack (ERP, banking, payroll)?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Financial operations terminology (close process, variance analysis, consolidation, audit trail)
- **Proof points**: Close cycle metrics, audit results, consolidation automation examples
- **Format**: Technical deep-dives, architecture discussions, implementation planning
- **Cadence**: Pre-adoption evaluation, implementation checkpoints, quarterly effectiveness reviews

## Decision Factors

- **Audit trail quality**: Must provide point-in-time reconstruction and immutable history
- **Control environment**: Cannot introduce control weaknesses or obscure activity
- **Integration requirements**: Must work with existing GL, banking, and reporting tools
- **Restatement handling**: Must handle corrections and period adjustments cleanly
- **Multi-entity support**: Must support consolidation and inter-company eliminations
- **Professional preparation**: Often engage external accountants; tool must support that workflow
- **Compliance requirements**: Must meet SOX, GAAP, or other regulatory requirements
