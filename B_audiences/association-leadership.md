# Association Leadership

## Description

Association leadership (executive directors, board members, education committee chairs) are the **gatekeepers for the B2B2B distribution channel**. They control access to member firms through recommended product listings, CPE programming, and event sponsorships. They don't use the product themselves — they provide distribution access to member firms.

## Primary Concerns

- **Member value**: Will this genuinely benefit our members or just be another vendor pitch?
- **Reputation risk**: If we endorse this and it fails, does it damage association credibility?
- **Revenue opportunity**: Does this partnership generate sponsorship, CPE, or licensing revenue?
- **Mission alignment**: Does this advance our mission of supporting the profession?
- **Member adoption**: Will members actually use this, or will it sit unused?
- **Competitive landscape**: Are other associations offering this? Will we fall behind?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Member benefit, professional development, association mission, partnership value
- **Proof points**: Pilot results from member firms, comparable association case studies, member testimonials
- **Format**: Partnership proposals, CPE content previews, board presentations
- **Cadence**: Quarterly partnership reviews, annual renewal discussions

## Decision Factors

- **Low risk to members**: Must demonstrate product works before broad endorsement
- **Revenue model clarity**: Partnership terms, sponsorship fees, member pricing must be transparent
- **CPE quality**: Content must meet CPE standards and provide genuine learning value
- **Member demand**: Evidence that members want or need this type of tool
- **No competition with members**: Tool must empower firms, not compete with their services
- **Pilot before endorsement**: Small-scale proof required before recommended product status
