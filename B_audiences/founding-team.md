# Founding Team

## Description

The founding team has worked for sweat equity for nearly two years and collectively owns 12% of the company. They are deeply invested in PowerModels' success, both financially and emotionally, and have made significant personal sacrifices to build the product and validate the business model.

## Primary Concerns

- **Equity value creation**: Is our 12% stake becoming valuable? What's dilution trajectory?
- **Sweat equity payoff**: When can we transition to market-rate compensation?
- **Product success**: Are we building something customers truly value?
- **Sustainable pace**: Can we maintain this intensity? What's path to work-life balance?
- **Role evolution**: Will there be room for growth as the company scales?
- **Mission fulfillment**: Are we making the impact we set out to make?
- **Team cohesion**: Are we aligned on vision, values, and execution?

## Communication Preferences

- **Language**: Product, customer impact, team accomplishments (shipped features, customer wins, problem solving)
- **Proof points**: Customer testimonials, product milestones, company valuation updates, hiring plans
- **Format**: All-hands meetings, weekly standups, transparent roadmap and metrics sharing
- **Cadence**: Daily collaboration, weekly team syncs, monthly all-hands, quarterly retrospectives

## Decision Factors

- **Mission alignment**: Does this decision advance our vision and values?
- **Equity impact**: How does this affect our ownership and future value?
- **Workload sustainability**: Can we execute without burning out?
- **Customer value**: Does this genuinely help customers or just chase metrics?
- **Team culture**: Does this preserve the culture and working environment we value?
- **Personal growth**: Does this create learning and advancement opportunities?
- **Compensation fairness**: When does sweat equity transition to cash? Is it equitable?

## Context

The founding team has:
- **Worked for sweat equity** for nearly 2 years (minimal or deferred cash compensation)
- **Collective ownership**: 12% of company equity
- **Deep commitment**: Demonstrated by extended period of below-market compensation
- **Skin in the game**: Personal financial sacrifice tied to company success
- **Product builders**: Hands-on execution of technical and business development
